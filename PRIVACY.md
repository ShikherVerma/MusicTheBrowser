# Fair Music Browser: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Fair Music Browser (launcher name "Fair Music") is made by Shikher Verma.

## The short version

We don't collect your data. No accounts, ads or analytics, and no server of
ours receives your browsing history, library or purchases.

## What stays on your device

The pages you visit, your music library, saved media and settings stay on
your device. You can delete songs in Music to free storage. Deleting the
app removes the library and browser data.

Your lifetime saved-song count is also kept outside the app's storage so
reinstalling doesn't reset the 1,000-song free allowance. On iPhone this is
one number in the device-only Keychain, not synced. On Android it is one
number in Google Play services Block Store, with cloud backup disabled.
If those device services aren't available, the app uses its local count.
Deleting songs doesn't reduce the count. We can't see this number.

## Purchases

The optional one-time unlimited-songs purchase uses Apple's App Store or
Google Play. The store processes payment and supplies the app with the
purchase entitlement. We don't receive payment details, run a receipt
server or receive your purchase history. The app caches the unlock on
your device so it can work offline. Restore purchase uses your store account.
The stores' own privacy policies apply to their services.

## Websites you visit

The app is a browser. Websites and services they use receive the requests
your browser makes, as with any browser. Their privacy policies apply.
The app doesn't bypass protected (DRM) streams. Only keep music you have
the right to keep.

## Open-source browser components

The app is built from Brave's open-source browser. Brave's usage pings and
anonymous usage statistics (P3A) are off. Wallet, News and VPN are disabled.
The app may fetch content-blocking lists and browser component updates
from Brave's servers. These requests don't include your browsing history
or library.

## Children

The app has unrestricted web access and is intended for adults, not children.
We don't knowingly collect anyone's personal data.
