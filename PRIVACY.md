# Music: The Browser: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Music: The Browser ("the app") is made by Shikher Verma. This policy
explains what happens to your data when you use it.

## The short version

We don't collect your data. The app has no accounts, no ads, and no
analytics, and we don't run servers that receive your data.

## What stays on your device

- The pages you visit and the media you play.
- Your music library: what you play is saved on your device so you can
  listen again offline.
- Your settings.

This data is stored only on your device. We can't see it. Deleting the
app deletes it, and you can remove items from your library at any time.

## Websites you visit

The app is a web browser. When you visit a website, that website (and
the services it uses) receives the requests your browser makes, as with
any browser. Their own privacy policies apply to what they collect.

## Built on Brave

The app is built from the open-source Brave browser. In this app:

- Brave's usage pings and anonymous usage statistics (P3A) are turned
  off.
- Brave Wallet, Brave News and Brave VPN are disabled.
- The app may download content-blocking lists and browser component
  updates from Brave's servers so ad and tracker blocking keeps working.
  These downloads don't include your browsing history or your library.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect data from anyone.
